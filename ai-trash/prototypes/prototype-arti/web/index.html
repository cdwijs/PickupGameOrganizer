Arti P2P Prototype — Web

Serverless peer-to-peer between two browsers, mirroring the native Arti prototype. Browsers can't run Tor onion services, so this uses a WebRTC data channel with manual copy/paste signaling instead of exchanging onion addresses. Works in Firefox, Chromium, and Safari.

Choose your role

One peer hosts the connection; the other joins by pasting the host's signal. Pick whichever fits — the two sides can swap freely.

Host

  1. Copy Your offer below and send it to the joiner.
  2. Paste the joiner's answer, then press Finish connect.

Join

  1. Paste the host's offer below, then press Create answer.
  2. Copy Your answer and send it back to the host.

Counter

Once connected, stream 1, 2, 3, … to the peer once per second. Press again to pause; press again to resume where it left off.
